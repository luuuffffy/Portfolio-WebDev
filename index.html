A LITTLE ABOUT ME 01 / 03

I’m Jeremy, a Computer Science student who enjoys turning ideas into clear, welcoming web experiences. I’m building my skills one project at a time and love seeing a design come to life in the browser.

I’m especially interested in front-end development: thoughtful layouts, responsive interfaces, and the JavaScript that makes a page feel useful and interactive. I also enjoy exploring how data and APIs can make websites more dynamic.

This portfolio brings together what I learned throughout the semester in my Web Development subject, including my Midterm and Finals lab activities. I’m continuing to build on these skills as I explore new ideas and create more projects.
